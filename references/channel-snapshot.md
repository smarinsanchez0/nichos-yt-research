## Key finding — real, not a guess

The channel's actual top performers over the last 12 months are **not**
the branded niche ("Futuro de la Longevidad" science/health content) at
all — they're short (0:23-1:29) videos selling digital-course packs and an
AUTODATA automotive-database subscription (#1-6 in the top-content table
above, 887-7,921 views each). The genuine longevity/science content that
matches the channel's current banner and "about" text is, so far, the
*weakest* performing material (80-634 views). This is a real strategic
tension to flag every time this skill runs recommendations for this
channel: the algorithm currently has mixed signals about what this channel
is "about," and doubling down on Season-1-style longevity content is a bet
on a pivot that hasn't paid off in view count yet — even though it may be
the right call for building a coherent, monetizable long-term identity
(see `SKILL.md` §Season 1). Don't paper over this tension in any
recommendation — surface it.
